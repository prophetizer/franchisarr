Unticking a server deletes the copy sync made there, and deleting a copy on its server unticks that server; deleting the original deletes its copies. Unticking a playlist stops syncing it and leaves its copies as ordinary playlists. A playlist of the same name that sync didn't make is never touched unless you link it. A Plex smart playlist follows its rules, so it's copied as it stands, one way. Music and photo playlists, and Franchisarr's own (see Franchisarr's), aren't listed.
