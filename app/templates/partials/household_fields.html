{#- The household settings (GLOSSARY.md: household setting): what the lists show, for everyone on this install. On Settings' Household section, and on the first-run step (0.77.0; they were the personal menu's "Preferences" before). -#}
Hide low-rated films
Example: at 6.0, Halloween: Resurrection (★4.6) and The NeverEnding Story III (★4.5) fold into a "below your rating floor" section on their collection's page, while Schindler's List (★8.6) stays. 0 turns it off. Films with fewer than ten votes are never hidden — unknown is not the same as bad.
Which directors get a page
films by. Example: on a library of 3,400 films, 5 gives about 150 directors — Spielberg, Nolan, the Coens — and 2 gives over 700, most of them people you own two films by and have never heard of.
TV films, specials and shorts in franchises and collections
Example: Wikidata files The Star Wars Holiday Special and LEGO Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Out under Star Wars. Off, they sit in a folded "TV films, specials or shorts" section on the franchise page; on, they count as gaps like any other film. TMDb collections follow it too: the Descendants films, made for Disney Channel, fold into "TV films" on their collection's page.
Shorts in a director's filmography
Example: Christopher Nolan directed Doodlebug, three minutes long and rated 6.5. Off, it folds into a "shorts" section on his page; on, it is a gap beside Insomnia. A film whose runtime TMDb doesn't know is never treated as a short.
