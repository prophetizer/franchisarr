TV films, specials and shorts in franchises
Example: Wikidata files The Star Wars Holiday Special and LEGO Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Out under Star Wars. Off, they sit in a folded "TV films, specials or shorts" section on the franchise page; on, they count as gaps like any other film.
Shorts in a director's filmography
Example: Christopher Nolan directed Doodlebug, three minutes long and rated 6.5. Off, it folds into a "shorts" section on his page; on, it is a gap beside Insomnia. A film whose runtime TMDb doesn't know is never treated as a short.
